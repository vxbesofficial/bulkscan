BulkScan

Film it. Flip it. It's scanned.

Scan whole binders, folders and books by recording a video of yourself flipping through the pages. BulkScan finds every page in the video, straightens and enhances it, and gives you a searchable PDF – all on your iPhone.

Coming to the App Store

Why video?

Every other scanner app makes you wait for the camera on each page. BulkScan records first and processes afterwards: you flip at your own pace, the app sorts it out when you're done. A 200-page lever-arch file takes a few minutes, not an afternoon.

Two pages per flip

Lay the folder flat. After every flip the back of the turned page and the front of the next one are both saved, in reading order. Rings in the middle, rings at the top – BulkScan finds the gutter itself.

Straight, clean pages

Perspective correction, automatic straightening to the text lines, even lighting (the phone's own shadow disappears), upright orientation and automatic splitting of sheets that carry two pages.

Searchable PDFs

On-device text recognition (English and German) makes every PDF searchable; copy text straight from a page with Live Text.

Private by design

No account, no cloud, no analytics. Your pages never leave your phone. The only network connection the app makes is to the App Store for the one-time purchase.

Smart review

Blank backs, duplicates and pages caught mid-air are hidden automatically. Re-crop, rotate, filter, split documents and share as PDF.

One-time purchase

Free for scans of up to 25 pages. Unlimited pages for a single purchase – no subscription.

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Works best with the phone held still (prop it up or use a small stand) under even light.
